## Placeholders (`images/products/placeholder/`)

SVG files we generated ourselves (public domain) that label where to put your own product photo.
They mark products with no freely-licensed photo available yet:

- insecticidal-soap.svg
- horticultural-oil.svg
- spinosad.svg
- beneficial-nematodes.svg
- kaolin-clay.svg
- isopropyl-alcohol.svg

**How to replace a placeholder:** take a photo of the product you bought (label visible), save it to `images/products/` with the same name (`.png` or `.jpg`), and update the path in the bug pages that reference it.
