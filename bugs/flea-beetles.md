# Flea Beetles (Phyllotreta, Epitrix spp.)

- **Type:** chewing
- **Houston timing:** April–June, again in early fall
- **Severity:** high on seedlings; low on established plants

## Identification

![Flea beetle on a leaf](../images/bugs/flea-beetles.jpg)
*Flea beetle on a leaf. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Phyllotreta_cruciferae_(07).jpg) — Whitney Cranshaw, CSU/Bugwood, CC BY 3.0 US.*

- Tiny (1.5–3 mm) beetles that jump like fleas when disturbed.
- Shiny black, brown, or striped.

### Look-alikes
- Cucumber beetles: much bigger (~6 mm), yellow with stripes/dots, don't dash-jump.

## Host plants

Eggplant, tomatoes, peppers, brassicas, beets, radish.

## Signs of damage

1. Lots of tiny round holes ("shot holes") in leaves.
2. Seedlings chewed to lace or bare stems in days.

## Lifecycle in Houston

Adults overwinter in soil litter, come out hungry in April — right at transplant time. Second generation in July–August. The spring cohort is the one that kills seedlings.

## Control (IPM)

### Monitor
- Weekly tap test over white paper; the jump tells you who.
- Check new transplants twice a week during the first weeks (Ref: Clemson).

### Prevent
- Row cover over young transplants — the main defense.
- Plant bigger, stronger transplants; they survive damage better.
- Control weeds and clear debris where adults overwinter (Refs: Clemson, UC IPM).

### Physical
- Row covers and trap crops (Refs: Clemson, UC IPM).

### Biological
- Tolerate light feeding on established plants; preserve predators (Ref: Clemson).
### Least-toxic products

![Kaolin clay (Surround)](../images/products/placeholder/kaolin-clay.svg) *Kaolin clay coating (e.g., Surround) — add your own product photo.*

- Kaolin clay, horticultural oil, or neem on young foliage (Refs: Clemson, UC IPM).

### Conventional (last resort)
- Only for severe seedling damage, per label (e.g., bifenthrin 7-day pre-harvest, carbaryl 14-day) (Ref: Clemson).

## Field notes (personal)

- Row cover over the eggplant saved the whole plantings from spring punch.
