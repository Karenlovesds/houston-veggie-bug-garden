# White Grubs (Scarabaeidae)

- **Type:** chewing (larvae eat roots)
- **Houston timing:** damage appears August–October; adults fly June–July
- **Severity:** medium–high for lawns

## Identification

![White grub larva](../images/bugs/white-grubs.jpg)
*C-shaped white grub larva. [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Борозняк_(лярва_хруща_травневого)_01.jpg) — Сарапулов, CC BY 4.0.*

- C-shaped white grubs (2–3 cm), brown head, six legs near the front.
- Curl into a C when handled.
- Adults: brown scarab beetles that buzz around porch lights in summer.

### Look-alikes
- Chinch bug damage: also brown patches, but the bugs are at the surface, not in the root zone.

## Host plants

Lawn grasses; also some vegetable roots.

## Signs of damage

1. Brown patches that don't recover when watered.
2. Lawn lifts up "like rolled carpet" — grubs are in the root zone (Ref: TX A&M).
3. Birds digging in the lawn (they hunt grubs).

## Lifecycle in Houston

Adults lay eggs in soil after June mating flights. Grubs feed on roots July–October, slow down in winter, resume in spring. Threshold: about 5–10 grubs per square foot (Ref: TX A&M).

## Control (IPM)

### Monitor
- Dig small plugs at the edge of dying turf, about 1 sq ft per 1,000 sq ft of lawn (Ref: TX A&M).
- Count grubs; treat when you hit the threshold.

### Prevent
- Time any preventive treatment near egg hatch — roughly June in south Texas — after the June-beetle flights end (Ref: TX A&M).

### Physical
- Water 1/4–1/2 inch the day before treatment, and water products in time (Ref: TX A&M).
### Biological

![Beneficial nematodes](../images/products/placeholder/beneficial-nematodes.svg) *Beneficial nematodes — add your own product photo.*

- Parasitic nematodes work — keep the soil moist and water them in well (Ref: TX A&M).
- Milky spore is NOT shown to be effective against Texas turf grubs (Ref: TX A&M).

### Traditional products
- Preventive: chlorantraniliprole, imidacloprid, thiamethoxam — most effective applied early.
- Curative (late season): carbaryl, trichlorfon — fast but short-lived. Follow the label (Ref: TX A&M).

## Field notes (personal)

- A flock of grackles descending on the bermuda in August = 40+ grubs per square foot. They did more control than any product.
