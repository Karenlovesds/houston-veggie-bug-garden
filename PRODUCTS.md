# Recommended Products (with photos)

The products this field guide recommends, one picture each. Product photos you see here are from Wikimedia Commons (credits: [images/CREDITS.md](images/CREDITS.md)). Where no free photo exists, a labeled placeholder marks the spot — take your own photo and drop it in.

| Product | What it's for | Photo |
|---|---|---|

| **Bt (Bacillus thuringiensis)** | Caterpillars — hornworms, cabbage worms, loopers, armyworms. Safe for natural enemies. | ![Bt product](images/products/bt-bacillus-thuringiensis.jpg) |

| **Insecticidal soap** | Aphids, whiteflies, mites — must contact the bug, cover undersides, repeat. | ![Insecticidal soap](images/products/placeholder/insecticidal-soap.svg) |
| **Horticultural oil** | Scale, mites, whiteflies, citrus psyllid (on flush). Dormant-season favorite. | ![Horticultural oil](images/products/placeholder/horticultural-oil.svg) |
| **Kaolin clay (Surround)** | Flea beetles and other chewing bugs: coats leaves, they won't feed. | ![Kaolin clay](images/products/placeholder/kaolin-clay.svg) |
| **Fire ant broadcast bait** | The Two-Step Method for fire ants — evening, dry, <90°F. | ![Fire ant bait](images/products/fire-ant-bait.jpg) |
| **Mosquito dunks / Bt israelensis** | Kill mosquito larvae in bird baths, rain barrels, any standing water you can't empty. | ![Mosquito dunks](images/products/mosquito-dunks.jpg) |

| **Beneficial nematodes** | White grubs in lawns — moist soil, water in well. | ![Beneficial nematodes](images/products/placeholder/beneficial-nematodes.svg) |
